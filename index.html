Automate Meetings & Follow-up

~45% OF TIME

Replace manual note-taking and follow-up drafting with AI that transcribes, summarizes, and extracts action items from your meetings.

Tools for the Job:

  • Fireflies.ai / Otter.ai: For automatic transcription.
  • Microsoft Copilot: For real-time summaries. (Watch Demo)

Streamline Client Paperwork

~16% OF TIME

Generate first drafts of routine client emails and reminders instantly. Digitize the entire paperwork lifecycle from drafting to signing.

Tools for the Job:

  • ChatGPT / Gemini: For drafting communications. (Watch Demo)
  • Docusign / PandaDoc: To automate sending and signing.

The Bottom Line: Reclaiming Your Focus

By automating just 30-60 minutes of administrative work per day, you could reclaim **3-5 hours per week**. This is time that can be directly reinvested into client acquisition, strategic planning, and personal development—the core activities that drive entrepreneurial growth.

Starter Prompt Library

Use these prompts with any modern AI assistant as a starting point. The key is to be clear and provide context.

Scenario Example Prompt
Summarize a Meeting Based on this meeting transcript, generate a 3-bullet summary and list all action items with assigned owners.
Draft a Follow-up Email Draft a polite but firm follow-up email to a client about outstanding paperwork. Mention the deadline is this Friday.
Brainstorm Marketing Ideas Generate 10 creative marketing slogans for a new estate planning service aimed at young families. The tone should be approachable.
Create a Template: New Lead Outreach Create a reusable prompt template for a concise outreach email to a new lead. Include placeholders for [Lead Name], [Company], [Noteworthy Sports Team Interest/Fact], and a clear call to action.
Create a Template: Onboarding Checklist Generate a comprehensive checklist template for onboarding a new client. Include sections for 'Financial Data Gathering', 'Long-Term Scope Definition', and 'Potential Product Lineups'.
Create a Template: Market Analysis Create a reusable prompt template for a weekly market analysis. It should have sections for 'Highlights' and 'Outlook', and a placeholder for the target medium: [Blog, Email, or LinkedIn].
Create a Template: Client Pipeline Emails Generate a set of 4 email prompt templates for different client pipeline stages: 1. Initial follow-up after a meeting. 2. Quarterly check-in. 3. Notification of a major fund change. 4. Response to a client-initiated question.
